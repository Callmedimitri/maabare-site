The Practice

A name. A passage. A practice.

01

From the word, the work.

Maabare takes its name from the Arabic معبر (maʿbar) — a crossing point. A passage between two places that would otherwise remain apart.

02

Two markets, one threshold.

For French sellers, MENA buyers sit behind language and channel barriers — scattered across networks, hard to reach in their language. For MENA investors, French sellers are reachable in theory but rarely surface in their inbox.

03

We sit precisely on that line.

Our work is to open doors — multilingual cold outreach, by email and LinkedIn DMs, that puts qualified contacts in front of you. The introduction is where our role ends and yours begins.

The Work

Two corridors.

Both directions are handled with the same single desk, the same languages, the same standard of care. No relays. No translation telephone.

N° 01

France → MENA

For French agencies and developers.

Multilingual cold outreach to MENA-based buyers — qualified prospects delivered straight to your inbox. Once the introduction lands, the conversation is yours to run.

  • Cold email and LinkedIn DM outreach in Arabic, French, and English
  • MENA prospect lists built and qualified — private investors, family offices, HNW individuals
  • Native-speaker outreach copy across every sequence
  • Qualified introductions delivered to your inbox — handoff is where we stop

N° 02

MENA → France

For MENA-based investors and buyers.

Multilingual cold outreach to French agencies, developers, and notaires — putting their inventory and projects on your radar. From the introduction forward, you handle it directly.

  • Cold email and LinkedIn DM outreach in French and English
  • Targeted lists of French agencies, developers, and notaires
  • Native-speaker outreach copy in your preferred language
  • Direct introductions to your inbox — you take the conversation from there
Approach

Four principles, one desk.

01

Discretion

Names, prices, and intent travel only between the parties who need to know.

02

Native fluency

Every conversation, document, and negotiation moves through French, Arabic, or English without translation drift.

03

Pre-qualified flow

Only buyers and listings that match — saving both sides from mismatched expectations.

04

Direct, not layered

A single point of contact across both jurisdictions. No relay chains. No diluted accountability.
